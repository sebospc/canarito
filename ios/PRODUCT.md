# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

## Stack

SwiftUI, iOS 26 and later, iPhone only. Swift package `CanaritoCore` for logic (tests with `swift test`), project generated by XcodeGen from `project.yml`. Spanish is the development language, English the second.

## Users

People who already run, or live with someone who runs, a Canarito server: an emulator-based receiver at a fixed place that gets Google's Android Earthquake Alerts. They carry an iPhone. They are not technical beyond scanning a QR the server shows. The first two users are the owner and his girlfriend; the app is free and open source for anyone who runs a server.

Situation that matters: the phone is on a table at night, locked, and a sound has to wake the person. The second situation: a person leaves home and wants to know, in one glance, whether they are still covered.

## Product Purpose

Pair the phone with a Canarito server, send the phone's rounded position so the server can place a receiver where the person is, and show the earthquake alert when it arrives as a push. Success is an alert that arrives, is seen in seconds, and an honest screen the rest of the time: covered, not covered, or server unreachable. Silence must never look like safety.

## Positioning

Not an alert system and not official. It carries Google's alerts to an iPhone, from a server the person or a friend runs. No account, no price, no company behind it.

## Operating Context

- Pairing: scan the QR that `canarito.py pair` shows on the server. One server per pairing.
- Pushes go through a small open-source relay (AWS Lambda) that holds the APNs key. It passes device token and alert text and stores neither.
- Background position: significant-change and region monitoring only, rounded before it leaves the phone.
- Critical Alerts are requested in code behind a flag (`CRITICAL_ALERTS`). The entitlement is not granted yet, so the app uses time-sensitive pushes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Privacy: the server operator is responsible for what the server stores. The app tells the person exactly what it sends (rounded position, device token) and to whom (the paired server). The privacy page is canarito.app/privacy (source site/build.py). No company name, no email.
- The app never claims what it cannot know. If the server cannot be reached, the screen says so and does not promise a fix.
- Bundle ID `app.canarito` (team BGKJDXR2L4, Push and Time Sensitive registered), App Store Connect record exists. Open: APNs key, Critical Alerts entitlement.
- Safety guidance on the alert screen quotes UNGRD (Colombia), word for word, with its source. No emergency number is shown, because the number depends on the country. Owner to confirm whether to keep the quote outside Colombia.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Canarito. The bird is the app icon and the small mark on the home screen. Never on an alert screen.
- Voice: "tú", everywhere, including the alert text ("Protégete ahora"). Plain, short, no exclamation marks.
- Alert text for people is in their language (es or en). Everything else in the app follows the same two languages.
- Always dark, on purpose: the app is used at night.

## Product Principles

1. Silence is not safety. Every unknown state ends in a visible warning, never in green.
2. Say only what is true for a self-hosted server. No "we", no promises of a fix.
3. One screen answers one question: am I covered right now.
4. The alert screen shows the fact and the protective action. Nothing else competes with it.
5. Free and open: nothing in the app asks for money, an account or an email.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Dynamic Type everywhere, including accessibility sizes (layouts switch from rows to stacks). VoiceOver reads every state and announces changes. Reduce Motion replaces slides and pulses with a cross-fade or nothing. Targets at least 44 pt. Text contrast at least 4.5:1.
